We're building **Ideal Solutions Pro** for contractors who work in the field — not behind a desk.

## What you get on day one

- **AI assistance** for estimates, materials, codes, and jobsite questions
- **Job Folder** for customers, jobs, photos, and reports
- **Materials search** across retail suppliers (and supply houses on higher tiers)
- **Getting Paid** through Cash App, Venmo, Square, and more when you're on Bossman Mode
- **Calendar** and shortcuts that match how trades actually run jobs

## Coming to iOS and Android

The app is heading to the App Store and Google Play soon. [Join the waitlist](/#waitlist) on our home page to get notified at launch.

## Plans for every stage

Start on **Helper Mode** with a free trial, then grow into **Side Job**, **Bossman**, or **Super Bossman** as your crew and workload scale. See [pricing](/pricing) for the full breakdown.
