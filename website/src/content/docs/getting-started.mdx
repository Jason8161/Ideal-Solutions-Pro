Ideal Solutions Pro is a mobile app for iOS and Android. This site is the home for downloads, pricing, and support until the stores go live.

## Before launch

1. [Join the waitlist](/#waitlist) to hear when the app is available.
2. Review [plans](/pricing) so you know which tier fits your work.
3. Read the [FAQ](/docs/faq) for common questions.

## After you install

1. Set up your **company profile** in Settings.
2. Open **Job Folder** and add your first customer and job.
3. Try **AI assistance** for a material list or code question.
4. When you're on Bossman Mode, enable **Getting Paid** and connect payment apps in Settings.

## Employee app

Bossman plans include crew features. A separate **Ideal Solutions Employee** build is available for clock-in, messages, and crew AI — ask your admin for the install link from your organization.
