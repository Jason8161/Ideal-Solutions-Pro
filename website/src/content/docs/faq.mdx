## When will the app be available?

We're preparing for App Store and Google Play release. Join the [waitlist](/#waitlist) for launch updates.

## Is there a free trial?

**Helper Mode** includes a 7-day trial of jobsite essentials before you choose a paid plan.

## Do I pay separately for AI?

No. AI is included in your subscription with daily limits on lower tiers. Bossman and Super Bossman include unlimited fair-use AI.

## Which payment apps work with Getting Paid?

Bossman Mode supports integrations such as Cash App, Venmo, and Square. Configure them in the app under Settings → Payment apps.

## Can my employees use the app?

Yes. Bossman plans support employees and a dedicated Employee app build for clock-in and crew tools.
