Subscriptions are purchased inside the app through the App Store or Google Play.

## Helper Mode (free trial)

- 5 AI questions per day
- Social media shortcuts
- Basic job folders and retail material search
- No employees, service calls, or Getting Paid

## Side Job / DIY Mode — $9.99/mo

- Photo-to-estimate uploads
- Calendar, job folders, and basic estimating
- Retail search (Home Depot, Lowe's, Ace)

## Bossman Mode — $19.99/mo (most popular)

- Unlimited AI (fair use)
- Employees, service calls, accounting, and Getting Paid
- Cash App, Venmo, Square
- Supply houses plus retail
- Crew AI for your team

## Super Bossman — $75/mo

- Everything in Bossman
- Unlimited image uploads and blueprint uploads
- Advanced AI on the jobsite
